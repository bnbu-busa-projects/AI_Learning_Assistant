# Asset: workbench-background-texture.png

## Purpose

Subtle first-screen workbench texture behind the artifact studio. It should make the product feel refined, warm, tactile, and editorial without becoming a hero image or decorative wallpaper.

## Prompt

Create a seamless premium workspace background texture for an academic AI artifact studio. The image should feel like warm dark graphite paper mixed with very faint vellum grain, barely visible drafting lines, and tiny document registration marks. Use a restrained palette of warm charcoal, ink black, ivory, muted sage, clay/terracotta, and a very small amount of amber. The texture must be quiet enough to sit behind dense UI controls and document previews. It should feel editorial and scholarly, not sci-fi. No text, no logos, no people, no objects, no classroom, no floating blobs, no gradient orbs.

## Constraints

- 2400 x 1600 px, tileable or visually seamless
- opaque background
- low contrast; must not compete with text or controls
- dominant colors should not read as pure dark blue/slate, purple, beige, espresso, or brown
- forbidden motifs: gradient blobs, bokeh, stock desk scenes, real university marks, fake charts, readable writing

- target path: `frontend/src/assets/textures/workbench-background-texture.png`
- target format: PNG
